## 10. tensorViz

Claude Code, Codex and Cursor can search that graph and read the measured shapes, so they fix model code from evidence instead of guesses. Or skip the install: the Model Explorer has graphs of 560+ models from recent papers to browse and discuss. Free and open source (MIT).
